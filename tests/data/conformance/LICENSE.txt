These files are the `input.jxl` of test cases of the JPEG XL conformance
repository (https://github.com/libjxl/conformance, testcases/<name>/input.jxl),
Copyright (c) the JPEG XL Project Authors, redistributed under its

photos, patches, progressive, spot, lossless_pfm, cmyk_layers, the long
animations) are not copied here for their size; `docs/decisions.md` records
how the decoder fares on all of them.
